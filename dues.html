National NSBE Dues

National dues are paid directly to the national NSBE organization and give you access to the national NSBE network, the annual convention, scholarships, and more.

Step-by-step instructions:

  1. Create an NSBE Account

    Go to nsbe.org and create a free account using your student email address.

  2. Search for UNCC Chapter

    Once logged in, search for the UNC Charlotte chapter and select it as your home chapter.

  3. Pay National Dues

    Navigate to the membership/dues section and pay the annual fee by credit or debit card directly on the NSBE website.

  4. Confirm with E-Board

    Send a screenshot of your payment confirmation to an e-board member or DM us on Instagram @UNCC_NSBE so we can verify your membership.

Note: National dues are separate from local dues. You need to pay both to be a full member in good standing. Questions? Email us at unccnsbe.pres@gmail.com
